Foundational Heritage

Frank & Maria: The Immigrant Spirit

In 1911, a 20-year-old immigrant named Frank Flocchini arrived in California from a small village in Lombardia, Italy. Armed with a relentless work ethic, Frank navigated a new country, establishing himself first as a regional share milker. Through rigorous saving, he achieved his dream of purchasing the family's Lakeville farm on April 16, 1919.

Regenerative Practices

A Decade of No-Till Viticulture: By leaving the soil undisturbed for over a decade, we protect the underground networks of mycorrhizal fungi. This translates directly into balanced vine canopies and complex flavor profiles.

Herbicide-Free Soil Armoring: We manage the vineyard floor using a combination of targeted Dorper sheep grazing and mechanical mowing. This lets a protective layer of natural mulch accumulate each season.

Biodiversity & Habitat Restoration: Beyond the vine rows, we are actively restoring the estate's natural ecosystem. By establishing native California oaks, multi-tiered hedgerows, and undertaking riparian creek bank restoration, we support indigenous wildlife and beneficial insect populations.
