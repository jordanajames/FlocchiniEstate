Our Heritage

The roots of the Flocchini Estate stretch back to April 16, 1919, when Frank and Maria Flocchini first purchased the Lakeville Ranch. For over a century, the land has been shaped by family hands, including Andrew, Nicholas, Anna, and Lena.

Today, our operations are guided by a deep respect for that legacy. Our leadership team honors this history while looking to the future: Andrew Flocchini serves as our Veteran Viticulturist and Elder Statesman, and Jordan James leads as Managing Partner of Fruit Sales and Operations.

Regenerative Viticulture

We believe great wine begins with living soil. Across our Chardonnay, Pinot Noir, and Syrah blocks, we have completely eliminated the use of herbicides.

Instead, we rely on nature's tools. We practice adaptive multi-paddock grazing, utilizing our flock of Dorper hair sheep for winter weed control. We apply heavy arborist wood-chip mulch and composted sheep manure to build active microbial life in the soil, ensuring our vines thrive naturally.
